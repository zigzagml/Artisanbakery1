Our Story: Returning to the Sacred Ritual of Bread

At Crust & Hearth, we believe that real bread cannot be rushed. It begins not with industrial shortcuts, but with deep respect for time, craft, and the living ecosystem of wild yeast.

Founded by master baker Julien Vance, our bakery was born from a simple desire: to resurrect the rustic traditions of old-world European boulangeries. Our mother sourdough starter—fondly named "Aura"—has been lovingly fed and nurtured daily for over 14 years.

We partner exclusively with independent organic millers who stone-grind locally grown heirloom grains. Each batch goes through a meticulous 36-hour cold fermentation process that naturally breaks down complex starches, creating our signature blistering golden crust, airy open crumb, and deeply complex caramelized aroma.

"Flour, water, sea salt, and patience. In their purest harmony, these four simple elements become sustenance for both body and spirit."

— Julien Vance, Founder & Master Baker
